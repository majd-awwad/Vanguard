# EARTHPULSE source ledger

`VERIFIED_RELATED_VISUAL` identifies working experiences with verified numerical inputs and a related NASA visualization. `VERIFIED_MATCHED` requires a verified frame-to-observation join. `PROPOSED_NOT_VERIFIED` identifies a candidate case. `BLOCKED` identifies an unavailable case. The stage maps derive from each packaged snapshot and the highlighted time. The NASA context stills retain their own dates and never drive the transport.

The temporal cases share a date-indexed transport with 0.9 seconds of playback per sample at 1×. SST maps 12–32 °C to 220–880 Hz on a fixed logarithmic scale. IMERG maps positive daily rates to one through six pulses, saturating at 60 mm/day. VIIRS maps window peak FRP to `0.19 × sqrt(min(FRP / 300 MW, 1))` gain at 185 Hz. The Studio keeps the scientific value and mapping parameters fixed when changing timbre, speed, mute or volume. On the maps, gridded dots are individual sampled cell centers and fire dots are detections. Coastlines use [Natural Earth 1:110m land v5.1.2](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/v5.1.2/geojson), public domain.

The seven MUR granule identifiers were checked against the [NASA CMR collection](https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/virtual-directory/collections/C1996881146-POCLOUD). The instrument links the selected nominal UTC time to its original granule metadata and to the NOAA numerical subset query.

NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio supplied the three [Earth Information Center daily visualization](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/gallery/daily-visualizations/) stills. The SST product was provided by JPL under NASA MEaSUREs. FIRMS imagery and data are credited to NASA LANCE FIRMS, part of NASA ESDIS. The rolling FIRMS URL changes over time, so the exact consumed response is retained in `science/atlas/`. Official 2026 challenge Resources-tab eligibility and exact SVS frame joins remain unverified. Manual screen-reader and human listening checks remain open.
